I am Zhiyu Lu, a Ph.D. candidate in Public Finance at the Central University of Finance and Economics (CUFE). My friends and colleagues also know me as Mark.

My research interests lie at the intersection of new institutional economics, the economics of innovation, and gender economics. I am particularly passionate about computational text analysis using Large Language Models to study institutional dynamics and their economic impacts.
