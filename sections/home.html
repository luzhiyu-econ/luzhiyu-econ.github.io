I am Zhiyu Lu, a Ph.D. Candidate in Public Finance at the Central University of Finance and Economics (CUFE). My friends and colleagues also know me as Mark.

My research interests lie at the intersection of political economy, public economics, and the economics of innovation. I use computational text analysis and large language models to recover high-dimensional semantic information from unstructured text and construct scalable measures for empirical economic research. I also study large language models as agents in economic experiments.

I maintain a Wiki to share practical notes on research and computing. I am currently writing a handbook on AI4Econ. I have also developed Python implementations of Stata commands and am a contributor to PyFixest.
