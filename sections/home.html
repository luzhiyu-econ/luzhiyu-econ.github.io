I am Zhiyu Lu, an Incoming Ph.D. Student in Public Finance at the Central University of Finance and Economics (CUFE). My friends and colleagues also know me as Mark.

My research fields are political economy, public economics, and the economics of innovation.

My methods include text as data, machine learning, and administrative data. I use computational text analysis and large language models to recover high-dimensional semantic information from unstructured text and construct scalable measures for empirical economic research.
